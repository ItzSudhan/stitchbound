**Stitchbound** is an original arena game played on a patchwork quilt. You steer a needle that trails red thread. Cross your own thread to close a loop: the loop cinches every pest inside it and darns the quilt's holes shut. Moths eat the quilt into holes, silverfish nip, button-backed beetles charge, earwig snippers cut your thread and walking pincushions fire fans of pins. Survive ten nights, beat the Yarn Wyrm on night 5 and the Moth Matriarch on night 10, and pick one of three notions (upgrades) after every night. Moths crave the gold-star heirloom patches, and each heirloom still whole at dawn pays a bonus. Sturdier pests sometimes drop spare spools that heal you. Lose all your spools or let the quilt unravel and the run is over. Your best score is kept. Everything is drawn in code on a 2D canvas and every sound is synthesised with WebAudio: no libraries, no image or sound files.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold click to fire pins, Space to snip-dash, P to pause, M to mute. On the title, Enter (or NEW RUN) starts a run, the arrow keys pick the mode and needle, and C opens the Sampler; on the results card, R (or PLAY AGAIN) sews again and Esc returns to the title.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
